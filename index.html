2. Using InWay without an account

You do not need to create an account or give a phone number to use InWay. Maps, GPS, navigation, radar alerts, nearby places and the AI assistant work without signing in.

Signing in with a phone number is optional. Some features, such as Smart Memory, require an account. The AI assistant is free for now; if we introduce paid AI features in the future, this policy will be updated before they start.

4. Microphone and voice

Voice input is captured only while you press the voice button. On Android, speech recognition for Uzbek runs on your device. Spoken answers are played from pre-recorded clips stored in the app; when a clip is not available, the text is sent to our server to generate speech (see section 5).
