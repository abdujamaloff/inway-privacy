Delete your InWay account

Applies to the InWay Android application (package uz.inway.app).

Draft. Items marked [TO CONFIRM] must be verified before publication.

You do not need an account to use InWay

InWay works without signing in. An account exists only if you chose to sign in with a phone number.

Option 1: delete in the app

  1. Open the app and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Hisobni o‘chirish (Delete account) and confirm.

This deletes your account record, your sessions and your phone-number record from our server. After that, you can keep using InWay without an account.

Option 2: request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app, email abdurashidabdujamalov13@gmail.com with the subject “InWay account deletion” and the phone number linked to your account. We will confirm the request and delete the account.

What is deleted

  • Your account record and your phone-number record (stored as a hash).
  • Your sign-in sessions and any unused one-time codes linked to your number.

Data kept only on your phone (favorites, history, playlists) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
